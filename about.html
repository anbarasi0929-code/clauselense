About ClauseLens

Making contract review easier to understand by identifying clauses that may need attention and explaining why they matter.

Our Project

ClauseLens is a contract risk-flagging application designed to help users review digital contracts. It extracts text from supported documents, checks for potential risk areas, and presents findings with explanations and relevant clause evidence.

Project Objectives

  • Allow users to upload supported contract documents.
  • Extract readable text from documents.
  • Identify clauses that may require further review.
  • Explain why a clause has been flagged.
  • Help users organize and review previous analyses.

Key Features

📄 Document Upload

Upload supported PDF, DOCX, JPG, JPEG, and PNG files.

🔍 Clause Analysis

Check contract text for potential risk indicators.

💡 Clear Explanations

Understand why a particular clause may deserve attention.

📚 Review History

View previous reviews when history storage is connected.

How It Works

  1. Upload a contract document.
  2. Extract text from the uploaded file.
  3. Check the extracted text against defined risk indicators and any requirements supplied by the user.
  4. Display potential risks with explanations and supporting text.
  5. Review the findings and decide whether professional advice is needed.

Technology Used

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: Python and Flask
  • Database: MySQL
  • Document processing: PyMuPDF and python-docx
  • OCR: Tesseract for supported scanned documents, when configured

Important Disclaimer

ClauseLens is a project intended to assist with contract review. Its flags are indicators for further examination, not definitive legal conclusions. It does not replace a qualified legal professional, and a flagged clause is not necessarily unlawful or unacceptable.
